03 // THE CODE

Five Lines,
Painted on the Wall

Every member is read these once, on the first night, and never again. After that they are assumed.

  1. I

    Arrive Before You Are Ready

    Motivation is weather. Attendance is architecture. The door opens and it does not wait.

  2. II

    Grit Is Raw Material

    Rage wins nothing on its own. It is ore. The work of this room is to smelt it into technique.

  3. III

    Control the Distance

    Every fight is a negotiation over one measurement. Whoever owns it rarely has to be brave.

  4. IV

    Respect the Loser

    They came. Half the town did not. When the bell goes, the opponent becomes a colleague again.

  5. V

    Leave the Floor Clean

    Wraps binned, ropes wiped, bags stilled. The room is inherited every morning by somebody else.

04 // THE EXCHANGE
